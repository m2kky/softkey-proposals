Project understanding

Bring service discovery, availability and booking confirmation into one clear journey. This is an illustrative project, not an agreed client brief.

02

Scope and deliverables

D01 / UX design

Design service selection, availability and confirmation screens with responsive layouts.

  • Acceptance: approved screens include error and success states.

D02 / Booking platform

Implement service listings, availability, booking and administration.

  • Acceptance: valid bookings succeed and unavailable slots cannot be booked.

D03 / Handover and training

Deliver source files, a short guide and an administration walkthrough.

  • Acceptance: an administrator can review and update a booking using the guide.

Assumptions and exclusions
